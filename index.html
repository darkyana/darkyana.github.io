About me

Most of my work lives at the point where a product stops being a pile of screens and starts being a system: shared components, coherent states, rules a team can actually follow. At BMW Group I lead eCommerce UX for the MyBMW and MINI apps and own the China section of the global app design system. Before that I managed a 12-designer team at Hujiang (CCtalk) and started out in enterprise UX at Cisco Webex.

Since early 2026 I've been running a small product studio with an unusual team structure. I'm the only human, the product owner, design lead, and final decision-maker, while AI agents cover PM, design, development, and QA. The interesting design problem is no longer the interface; it's the contract between human intent and machine execution: specs, vocabulary, acceptance, audit trails. That work lives on the Experiments page.

I work in Chinese and English, design in Figma, and build hands-on with React, Vue, and TypeScript when a prototype is the fastest way to answer a question.
